01 — Our Story & Heritage

About Golden Properties

Golden Properties Pvt. Ltd. was established over four decades ago with a pioneering vision to bring institutional transparency, bespoke advisory, and uncompromising excellence to India’s prime real estate landscape. What started as an ambitious advisory office has grown into one of the most trusted and respected real estate firms across Gurugram, Delhi NCR, and beyond.

Through consistent hard work, unyielding integrity, and a client-first approach, the company has built a distinguished reputation for reliability and market excellence. Today, Golden Properties proudly operates with a robust network of seasoned consultants and real estate professionals, actively consulting across 200+ landmark residential and commercial projects.

Our platform not only showcases top-tier developments but also educates and empowers buyers and investors about each project’s unique lifestyle benefits, valuation metrics, and growth potential. Backed by an experienced team of senior advisors, we guide clients seamlessly from their initial inquiry all the way to private site tours, legal documentation, deal closure, and registration.

We are proud channel partners with North India’s and the country's most iconic developers — including DLF Limited, Godrej Properties, Emaar India, Signature Global, M3M India, BPTP, Puri Constructions, Birla Estates, Adani Realty, Central Park, Conscient, and Sobha.

And we’re not stopping there — our forthcoming real estate intelligence platform and digital suite will bring the latest market trends, new launch updates, and institutional property insights directly to our clients’ fingertips.

Transparent Dealings & Complete Fiduciary Care — 100% verified documentation, transparent commission structures, and zero hidden clauses.

Proprietary Market Intelligence & Portfolio Advisory — Comprehensive CRM tools, rental yield forecasting, and bespoke wealth management support.

Trusted by Industry Leaders — Award-winning channel partner network officially recognized by India’s tier-1 developer conglomerates.
